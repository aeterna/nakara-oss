AETERNA Starter Kit

Seven base resources for running your own FiveM server. Use one, or all of them. The coming 0.2 adds five more (see below).

What is in it

  • aeterna_core: shared focus handling, locale, time and client storage.
  • aeterna_uikit: panels, confirm, input, progress, skill check and a menu.
  • aeterna_notify: toasts, announcements and a message of the day.
  • aeterna_layout: each player can move and scale HUD parts, including the game minimap.
  • aeterna_hudkit: status bars, vehicle panel, street name and minimap.
  • aeterna_phonekit: a phone with four built-in apps and an API for your own.
  • aeterna_invkit: a personal stash and timed item use on top of ox_inventory.

Coming in 0.2

0.2 is not out yet. Everything in this section comes from specs written before the code, and details can change before release. The seven packages above go to 0.2.0, and five new packages start at 0.1.0, which makes twelve.

aeterna_radial · radial menu

A ring of 4 to 8 slots in the middle of the screen, where a slot can open a second ring. Other resources add and remove slots by export, with conditions that decide when each one shows. For a slot that affects other players, the server checks the conditions again before acting. The kit ships an empty menu for your server to fill. Mouse or gamepad, no default key.

aeterna_loading · loading screen

Tells players what is really happening while they join: the map loading, scripts starting (as a percentage) and the wait for their character. It shows your server's name, a background image and rotating tips, all from one config file. It loads nothing from other hosts.

aeterna_testkit · test the whole kit

One button runs every self-test the packages registered with aeterna_core. It walks the presser through each test that needs a person's eyes, can invite a second player for two-player tests, and writes a JSON and Markdown report you can attach to an issue as it is. The report carries no player names, identifiers or IPs. Needs the ACE aeterna.testkit.run.

aeterna_phonecall · calls and messages

Phone numbers, text messages and voice calls as two apps inside the aeterna_phonekit phone. Numbers and messages are stored on the server, where players cannot edit them. On FiveM Enhanced calls use FXServer's own voice channels; on Legacy they use pma-voice when it is started. With no way to call, messages still work.

aeterna_camera · camera and gallery

A camera app (selfie and back camera, a shutter) and a gallery inside the phone. Photos are taken when screenshot-basic is started and saved on your server's disk, in the resource's own folder. The kit never uploads a photo anywhere. To send photos elsewhere, have your own resource listen to aeterna_camera:saved.

Three theme presets

0.2 adds the presets asphalt (default), paper (light) and night-market (dark, orange accent), chosen with setr aeterna_theme_preset. Try them in the theme playground.

Install

Download the zips, unpack them into resources/[aeterna_kit]/, then add this after your framework:

# server.cfg
setr aeterna_locale "en"
ensure aeterna_core
ensure aeterna_uikit
ensure aeterna_notify
ensure aeterna_layout
ensure aeterna_hudkit
ensure aeterna_phonekit
ensure aeterna_invkit

No package sets a default key. Players bind keys in Settings → Key Bindings → FiveM.

Tested with

FXServer Enhanced, game build 3788, with qbx_core 1.23.0, ox_lib 3.30.6, oxmysql 2.14.3 and ox_inventory 2.44.0. Other versions are not tested.

Your server, your look

The default theme is plain on purpose. Point the aeterna_theme convar at your own stylesheet to change colours, radius and font across every screen. Every release is built in CI and carries a provenance attestation you can verify before installing. From 0.2 you can also pick one of three presets (theme playground).
